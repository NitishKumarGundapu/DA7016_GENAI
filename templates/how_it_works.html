THE PIPELINE

From answer sheets to defensible feedback.

Four inputs, one LangGraph workflow, and a review sheet built for human judgment.

01

The graph at a glance

Top-level pipeline + per-question loop
02

What you provide

Four inputs, one assessment

Question paper *

The questions and their maximum marks. PDF, image, TXT, or MD.

Model answers *

One reference answer per question. Upload a file or paste the text.

Student pages *

One or more answer sheets. Reorder pages before grading so the transcript stays in order.

Rubric OPTIONAL

Criterion names, descriptions, and explicit marks. Without it, each question is graded as a whole.

03

What happens inside

From first page to final feedback
1

Read the material

Text is read directly. PDFs and images go to OpenRouter OCR, which preserves page order and turns diagrams, graphs, and tables into structured text.

2

Understand the standard

The question paper, model answers, and optional rubric are aligned into one spec. Missing or contradictory mark allocations stop the run before grading.

3

Connect the answers

The transcript is split into per-question answers. Missing, duplicated, contradictory, or ungrounded answers are withheld for human review.

4

Grade & verify

Each criterion needs a verbatim evidence quote. Code checks bounds and grounding, an optional critic reviews fairness, and failed checks retry.

5

Add it up

Marks and percentages are calculated in code, never by the model. Every grade is ordered back into question-paper order.

6

Make feedback matter

The report adds overall feedback and up to three study priorities in the paper's language, then renders Markdown for export.

04

Grading safeguards

Checks before marks
  • Every awarded criterion must quote the student's answer, and the quote is fuzzy-matched in code.
  • Marks are clamped to rubric bounds; totals and percentages are summed in code.
  • The critic checks for unearned credit and penalties for valid alternatives.
  • Unattempted questions score zero without an LLM call and remain in the paper total.
  • Uncertain transcription is flagged for review, never guessed or completed.
  • Questions that still fail verification are flagged in the report instead of silently accepted.
05

What you get

Review, print, export
  • A review sheet with totals, overall feedback, study priorities, and warnings.
  • Search, filters, sorting, and jump links across every question.
  • Criterion evidence, strengths, gaps, and concrete next steps.
  • Markdown, JSON, and CSV downloads, plus print for the filtered list.
  • Results held in the browser tab until you clear them or close it.
